Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Loan Calculator: EMI & Interest ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

By downloading, accessing, or using Loan Calculator: EMI & Interest, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms, please do not use the application.

1. Overview & Core Commitment

We built Loan Calculator: EMI & Interest as an offline-first utility tool.

  • Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, sync, or share any personal information, financial inputs (such as loan amounts, interest rates, or tenure), or calculations you enter into the app.
  • Offline Functionality: All calculations are processed locally on your device. The app does not require an active internet connection to perform its core functions.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Because our app operates entirely on your device, we never ask for or collect:

  • Personal Identifiable Information: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • Financial Data: Loan details, EMI amounts, income details, or banking information. All data typed into the calculator stays strictly on your phone or tablet.
  • Device Contacts, Photos, or Location: We do not request permissions to access your contacts, camera, photo library, or GPS coordinates.

3. Third-Party Services & Monetization (Ads)

The only reason Loan Calculator: EMI & Interest requires an internet connection (when available) is to display advertisements to support the free availability of the app.

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID / AAID or IDFA)
